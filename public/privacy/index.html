Privacy notice

Clear data boundaries.

This notice explains what Intelligent Decisions Interactive (“IDI,” “we,” “us”) processes when you visit our site, apply for a beta, or use an IDI product. Product-specific differences are called out below.

Effective and last updated: September 12, 2026

What we collect and why

We process information you provide, information created while you use a product, payment and entitlement records, and limited technical request data needed to deliver and protect the service. Depending on the feature, this can include an email address, account and organization details, uploaded or pasted business material, analysis results, file metadata, purchase identifiers, beta application answers, and support correspondence.

We use that information to provide requested features, authenticate accounts, prevent abuse, process purchases, deliver transactional messages, maintain records, improve reliability, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information or use product content for advertising.

Payment card boundary. Card and payment-method details are collected by Stripe through its payment interface. IDI receives transaction, status, tax, and entitlement information, but does not store complete card numbers.

Our products use essential session cookies or browser storage for authentication, security, and in-progress application state. Cloudflare Turnstile may process browser and request signals to distinguish people from abusive traffic. We have not implemented advertising cookies on these product pages.

Product-specific processing

ADBridge

The public ADBridge page receives ordinary website request data. If you initiate an Enterprise Early Access inquiry, your email client sends the message and information you choose to provide. The separately installed Developer and Enterprise software does not run through this public website; its package, license, enterprise agreement, and deployment configuration may supply additional terms and notices.

CourierIQ

Beta applications include your name, contact email, Google Play account email, optional state, Android device, delivery platforms, approximate weekly delivery volume, interest statement, consent record, submission metadata, review status, and administrative or email-delivery history. We use this information to assess eligibility, manage tester access, contact applicants, and operate the beta.

Chargeback Studio

Chargeback Studio stores account, organization, dispute, deadline, processor, amount, recovery, factual-summary, evidence-file, response-pack, event, and purchase information needed to maintain the merchant workspace. Evidence files are placed in private storage and access is restricted through authenticated account and organization controls. Banks and processors—not IDI—decide dispute outcomes.

Revenue Leak Finder

Raw CSV rows are parsed and reconciled in your browser. When a signed-in user saves a report, the service receives aggregate totals, source file names, row and issue counts, value-at-risk summaries, account details, history, and credit events. Raw CSV rows, customer emails, transaction IDs, and downloaded reports are not uploaded or stored by Revenue Leak Finder.

BidLens

An uploaded procurement document or pasted source is processed in memory for the analysis you request and is not saved by IDI. The source content is sent to OpenAI for analysis. Your workspace may retain the generated report, source file name and size, model identifier, account details, history, and credit events.

ScopeFence

ScopeFence currently retains saved scope agreements, client requests, analyses, generated change-order material, account details, history, and credit events so the workspace can be reopened. The relevant scope, request, and hourly-rate context is sent to OpenAI for the analysis you request. Self-service deletion is not yet available; request deletion using the contact below. ScopeFence will not be designated Live until required data controls are in place.

OpenAI processing. IDI sets store: false on BidLens and ScopeFence Responses API requests, so the response is not stored as retrievable OpenAI application state. OpenAI’s default abuse-monitoring logs may still retain customer content for up to 30 days unless different approved data controls apply. IDI does not permit OpenAI to train its models on API data by default.

Service providers

We use providers only for the functions needed to operate the site and products:

  • Cloudflare for hosting, request delivery, security, and Turnstile abuse prevention.
  • Supabase for product authentication, databases, and private file storage.
  • Stripe for checkout, payment processing, tax calculation, and transaction status.
  • Resend for beta and transactional email delivery.
  • OpenAI for requested BidLens and ScopeFence analysis.

These providers process information under their own contracts and privacy terms. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the service, or as part of a business transaction subject to appropriate safeguards.

Retention and deletion

We keep information only as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, maintain security and transaction records, resolve disputes, and meet accounting or legal obligations. The period varies by product and record type. Beta application data is retained while it remains useful to operate or evaluate the beta. Account and workspace data is retained while the account is active and until deletion is completed, subject to required transaction, security, backup, or legal records.

Where self-service deletion is not available, email a deletion request. We will verify the requester and remove or de-identify eligible records from active systems. Limited records may be retained where required by law, needed to document a transaction or prevent abuse, or present in backups until those backups cycle.

Your choices and requests

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a copy of personal information. You may also withdraw optional communications consent. These rights can be limited by identity verification, legal exceptions, and records we must retain. Send privacy and deletion requests to development@intelligentdecisions.io and identify the relevant product and account email.

Security and children

We use access controls, encryption in transit, restricted provider credentials, and monitoring appropriate to the service. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Do not send passwords, complete payment-card data, or unnecessary sensitive information through email support.

IDI’s business and developer products are not directed to children. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account, purchase a product, or apply for a beta.

Contact us

For privacy, access, correction, or deletion requests, email development@intelligentdecisions.io. For general product support, visit Support. We may update this notice as products and providers change; the date above identifies the current version.
