Privacy and deletion

Identify the product and the account email whose data you want us to locate.

Request data help

Beta, pre-launch, and Early Access products do not have a published response-time guarantee. Any Enterprise service level must be stated in a signed agreement.

What to include

  • Product name and the email used for the account or beta application.
  • Approximate date and time, including your time zone.
  • The step you were completing and the result you expected.
  • The visible error message, transaction identifier, or a redacted screenshot when relevant.
  • Browser and device type for website issues, or the authorized ADBridge component version for deployment issues.

Do not email secrets. Never send passwords, one-time codes, API keys, complete card data, private signing keys, or customer evidence files unless support specifically provides an approved secure method.

Payment and entitlement issues

Include the product name, purchaser email, approximate amount and date, and Stripe receipt or payment identifier if available. Do not send a full card number. IDI grants paid access only after server-side payment confirmation, so a delayed webhook can require reconciliation.

Data deletion

ScopeFence provides in-product controls for deleting saved agreements, individual checks, all check history, or the entire ScopeFence workspace. Other products may still require a verified manual request. Email development@intelligentdecisions.io with the product and account email. We will verify control of the account before acting. Transaction, fraud-prevention, security, or legal records may need to be retained as described in the Privacy Notice.

Security reports

Send a concise description, affected URL or component, reproducible steps, and potential impact to development@intelligentdecisions.io. Do not access other users’ information, disrupt service, or publish sensitive details while we investigate.
